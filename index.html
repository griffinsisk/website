About

I'm a Senior Solutions Engineer at CloudZero, where I help large companies understand and control what they spend on cloud and AI. Day to day that means sitting with engineering, finance, and AI platform teams as they work to visualize and understand how Claude, OpenAI, Cursor, Copilot, and other AI usage applies back to the people and products driving it, alongside the outcomes they're delivering. Six years of technical pre-sales at VMware, Broadcom, and CloudZero taught me how to earn trust with engineers, translate complexity for executives, and close competitive deals. More recently I've learned the hardest problems in enterprise AI sit outside the models, in trustworthy data, proper context, and numbers people will actually act on.

The pattern in my best work is the same whether I'm selling or building: get into the customer's real data fast, be honest about what works and what doesn't, and ship something they can use this week. That approach has cleared compliance blockers other vendors walked away from, turned AI cost visibility into a reason for executives to buy, and cut our evaluation cycle in half. I'm looking for a role where I can do more of exactly that, embedded with customers at a company building the AI frontier.

Python · AWS Lambda · Amazon S3 · AWS Secrets Manager

  • Idempotent by design: a deterministic ID built from the source version and exchange rate means a month is only reprocessed when the data or the rate changes.
  • Exact decimal arithmetic for money, verified by a round-trip test that must match the original to the cent.
  • Written by hand, without AI coding assistance.
